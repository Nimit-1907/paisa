# Design review — Phase 10

Phases 1–9 were built quickly. This phase added no features. It looked at every
screen and asked one question: does this help somebody log money fast, or
understand where it went? What could not answer was changed or deleted.

Every change below has the reason it was made next to it. Accessibility has
its own file: [accessibility.md](accessibility.md).

Screenshots at 360px sit in `docs/screenshots/` as `before-*` and `after-*`
for Records, Analysis, Budgets, Accounts and Recurring, plus the Add Record
sheet, the login screen and the new landing page. They are deliberately not in
git — a PNG keeps every revision of itself in history forever — so the folder
is ignored and they are regenerated by driving the built app at 360×780.

---

## 1. What was wrong, screen by screen

Audited at 360×780 and 1280×800, in both themes, against the demo account
(which has real records on it — an empty screen passes everything and proves
nothing).

### Records

| Finding | Change | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Three equal bold numbers — income, expense, net — in a 3-column grid. Nothing was the answer. | One focal number: **Left this month**, with income and out beneath it at `text-xs`. | The question somebody opens this app with is "how much is left", and the screen was answering three questions at the same volume. |
| Income and expense were told apart by colour alone. | Added `ArrowDownLeft` / `ArrowUpRight` beside each. | Colour-blind users and anyone in bright sunlight get the same information (WCAG 1.4.1). |
| Heading said "Net" whether the month was up or down. | Reads **Left this month** or **Overspent this month**. | A negative number with a neutral label makes you do the interpreting. |

### Budgets

| Finding | Change | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The percentage used was the biggest thing in the card; the amount left was secondary and the per-day figure was a truncated grey afterthought. | Remaining is now `text-3xl`; `₹1,466.52/day for 17 more days` is promoted to medium foreground text; the ring is 72px with the percentage at `text-sm`. | "What can I spend today" is the decision this screen exists to support. 36% is trivia. |
| In a budget row, the amount left truncated to `₹1,896.00…` so the "includes ₹750.00 scheduled" note could keep its full width. | The amount is `shrink-0`; the scheduled note truncates instead. | The number is the answer and the note is the footnote; they were the wrong way round. |
| The ring and the bars animated for 500ms. | 200ms, and only under `motion-safe:`. | Half a second of movement on every render is noticed, not read. |

### Analysis

| Finding | Change | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The screen opened with a donut. A chart shows the shape of a month; it does not say what changed. | A sentence leads: *"Food is your biggest expense this month, 18% higher than last month."* | Reading one sentence is faster than reading a chart, and it is the thing somebody came to find out. |
| | `src/lib/insight.ts` is pure and unit-tested (6 tests), including amounts above 2^53. | A sentence that says "higher" when it was lower is worse than no sentence. |
| Charts had no text alternative. | "View as table" under the bar chart and the balance chart. | See [accessibility.md](accessibility.md). The donut already had a list of categories with shares and amounts, so it did not need a second one. |

### Add record

| Finding | Change | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The sheet opened on whichever account sorted first and an empty category grid: 7 taps for a typical expense. | `entry_defaults()` (new migration) returns the account used last and the categories usually picked at this hour; the sheet opens with the account chosen and a **Usually in the evening** row above the grid. | Measured: **4 steps** (FAB → amount → category → save), 6 raw taps for ₹250 of which 3 are amount digits. Worked out from the records themselves rather than stored on the device, so it is still right on a second phone. |
| The amount display was a `<div>` with an `onClick` that focused a hidden input. | It is a `<label>`. | A label focuses its own control, from a mouse and from the keyboard, with no JavaScript. |
| Typing `1200/3` on a physical keyboard produced an error. | `/` and `*` are translated to `÷` and `×`. | Those are what the keys are labelled. |
| Enter did nothing. | Enter works a sum out, then saves. | The keypad is not inside a form, so the one key everyone presses was dead. |

### Everywhere else

| Finding | Change | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `text-[11px]` appeared nine times across seven files. | One token, `--text-label`. | Nine one-offs is how a type scale stops being a scale. |
| Eight shadcn primitives were installed and never imported (`badge`, `card`, `scroll-area`, `select`, `separator`, `sheet`, `tabs`, `tooltip`). | Deleted. | Confirmed zero references each. They were scaffolding, not code. |
| Empty states on Analysis, Recurring and an account's detail page described the situation and offered nothing to do. | Each has one button, `AddRecordButton`. | An empty screen should say what to do next, in a button, not point at a floating "+" in a sentence. |

### Looks finished but does not work

Three of these. All now fixed; all were invisible to a type checker and to the
existing tests.

1. **The bottom nav labels rendered at 16px, not 11px.** `cn()` is
   clsx + tailwind-merge, and tailwind-merge resolves conflicts from a table of
   known class groups. `text-label` is a project token, so it was not in the
   table and was read as a text *colour* — in the nav, where the size and the
   active colour arrive in the same `cn()` call, `text-primary` deleted it. The
   labels then collided at 320px ("RecordsAnalysisBudgetsAccounts"). Fixed by
   registering the token with `createCn` in `src/lib/utils.ts`.
2. **The recharts surfaces were focusable and hidden at the same time.** Keyboard
   users tabbed into something a screen reader was told to ignore. `tabIndex={-1}`.
3. **A `<label>` nested inside a `<label>`** in the budget sheet's "Apply to all
   future months" row — invalid HTML, and the inner one labelled nothing.

## 2. Design tokens

Every ratio below is measured from the tokens in `src/app/globals.css`
(oklch → sRGB → WCAG contrast), not estimated. Full output:
`docs/accessibility.md`.

| Token | Light | Dark | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `--background` | `oklch(0.985 0.003 247)` | `oklch(0.17 0.015 258)` | The page |
| `--foreground` | `oklch(0.21 0.02 255)` | `oklch(0.96 0.005 250)` | Body text — 16.97:1 / 17.03:1 |
| `--card` | `oklch(1 0 0)` | `oklch(0.215 0.018 258)` | Every panel |
| `--muted-foreground` | `oklch(0.5 0.018 255)` | `oklch(0.72 0.015 255)` | Secondary text — 5.74:1 / 7.71:1 |
| `--primary` | `oklch(0.47 0.09 192)` | `oklch(0.78 0.11 185)` | Buttons, the FAB, the active tab |
| `--income` | `oklch(0.48 0.12 157)` | `oklch(0.8 0.15 158)` | Money in — 5.86:1 / 10.87:1 |
| `--expense` | `oklch(0.51 0.18 20)` | `oklch(0.74 0.16 22)` | Money out — 6.06:1 / 7.71:1 |
| `--transfer` | `oklch(0.5 0.15 258)` | `oklch(0.76 0.12 255)` | Between your own accounts |
| `--warning` | `oklch(0.54 0.13 68)` | `oklch(0.81 0.13 75)` | A budget at 80–100% |
| `--destructive` | `oklch(0.52 0.19 24)` | `oklch(0.68 0.17 24)` | Errors and delete |
| `--border` | `oklch(0.915 0.008 250)` | `oklch(1 0 0 / 12%)` | A hairline between surfaces — decoration |
| `--input` | `oklch(0.64 0.012 250)` | `oklch(1 0 0 / 36%)` | The edge of a control — 3.36:1 / 3.33:1 |
| `--ring` | `oklch(0.55 0.08 192)` | `oklch(0.6 0.08 190)` | Focus — 4.47:1 / 5.04:1 |
| `--chart-1…5` | `0.48–0.58` lightness | `0.74–0.8` lightness | Slices with no category colour — all ≥ 4.4:1 |

**Type scale**: `--text-label` 0.6875rem · `text-xs` 0.75 · `text-sm` 0.875 ·
`text-base` 1 · `text-lg` 1.125 · `text-3xl` 1.875. Headings are `font-semibold`
with `tracking-tight`; amounts carry `.amount` (`tabular-nums`,
`letter-spacing: -0.01em`) so columns of figures line up.

**Spacing**: `space-y-4` between sections, `p-3`/`p-4` inside a card,
`gap-1.5` inside a control group, `rounded-2xl` for cards and `rounded-xl` for
controls. Tap targets are at least 44px (`h-11`), asserted by a test.

`--input` was `oklch(0.915 …)`, the same hairline as `--border`, which gave a
text field's edge 1.29:1 against a card. It is darker now because the border is
the only thing that says a control is there (WCAG 1.4.11 wants 3:1).
`--border` stays light on purpose: a card's edge is decoration, and the content
inside it is what identifies the card.

## 3. Motion

- A global `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` block in `globals.css`
  cuts every transition and animation to 0.01ms. Radix and vaul ship their own
  keyframes, so handling this component by component would have missed the
  dialog that flies in and the sheet that slides up — the exact two things
  somebody turns this setting off for.
- The spinner is exempt: it is a status, not decoration, and freezing it
  mid-turn leaves a saving button looking stuck.
- Transitions are 200ms or less and move `transform`, `opacity`, `color` or a
  single SVG property. Nothing animates `height` or `width` except the budget
  bar, which is the thing being measured.
- recharts entry animation is off everywhere (`isAnimationActive={false}`),
  not only under reduced motion: a chart that grows out of the axis delays the
  number you came to read.
- An e2e test asserts that nothing on a loaded screen has a transition or
  animation longer than 1ms while reduced motion is on.

## 4. The public pages

New `(marketing)` route group: `/`, `/features`, `/faq`, `/privacy`. All four
are static, Server Components only, and share one content file so the page and
the structured data cannot drift apart.

- `/` leads with "Know where the month went.", then the four taps named one by
  one, then what you get and why it can be trusted. No screenshots: an image
  above the fold is the usual way to lose LCP, and the claim is checkable in
  words.
- `SoftwareApplication` JSON-LD on `/`, `FAQPage` on `/faq` — built from the
  same arrays the pages render, and a test asserts every answer in the markup
  is visible on the page, which is what the rich result requires.
- `sitemap.ts` lists the four public pages. `robots.ts` disallows the
  signed-in and auth paths, which would otherwise be indexed as ten copies of
  the login screen.
- The OG card is generated, and draws the rupee as an SVG path rather than
  typing "₹": the renderer only has the fonts it is given, and that one glyph
  made the build reach for the network.
- A signed-in visitor hitting `/` is redirected to `/records` in middleware,
  which keeps the page static. The other three stay readable either way —
  somebody logged in who taps "Privacy" wants the privacy page.
- The toast host and the service-worker listener were moved out of the root
  layout and then moved back. First Load JS for `/` was 109kB either way —
  104kB of that is Next's own runtime, and sonner was already split out of the
  landing page's chunk — so the move bought nothing measurable and left four
  shells to remember instead of one. The only client component the public
  pages mount is the theme provider, which has to be at the root to set the
  class on `<html>` before the first paint.

**Measured** (Lighthouse 11.7.1, mobile preset, production build, on a
developer laptop — so these are noisy; `/` is the median of three runs and the
others are a range across two):

| Page | Performance | Accessibility | Best practices | SEO | LCP | CLS | TBT |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | 97 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 2.45s | 0 | 117ms |
| `/features` | 98 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 2.4–2.5s | 0 | 30–40ms |
| `/faq` | 96–98 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 2.5s | 0 | 30–130ms |
| `/privacy` | 96–97 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 2.5–2.6s | 0 | 30–80ms |

Desktop preset on `/` is 100 across all four categories. Unthrottled, LCP is
224ms and the element is the lead paragraph; the mobile figure is simulated
slow 4G with a 4× CPU penalty, which is what the target of 2.5s was set
against. `/privacy` measured 2.6s on one of two runs — the page is a column of
text with no images, so what is being timed is Next's own runtime arriving
over a simulated slow connection, not anything on the page.

## 5. What was deliberately left alone

- **`--border` at 1.23:1.** It is a hairline between a card and the page, not
  the identifying edge of a control. Raising it to 3:1 would turn every list
  into a grid of boxes.
- **The rule history empty state** ("Nothing created yet") has no button. The
  rule fills it by itself on its due date; the only honest action would be
  "Pay now", which is already on the Records screen when something is due.
- **`isAnimationActive={false}` rather than a reduced-motion check** on the
  charts. Keeping entry animation for everyone else would mean maintaining two
  behaviours for an effect nobody asked for.
- **The suggestion row only appears for a new expense.** Editing already has a
  category, and income has too few categories for a shortcut to shorten
  anything.
